1. **Ghost Rider** is a Marvel Comics antihero, usually portrayed as a human bonded with a supernatural spirit of vengeance.
2. He’s known for his flaming skull, motorcycle, and ability to punish the wicked with a supernatural gaze called the **Penance Stare**.
3. The best-known Ghost Riders include **Johnny Blaze** and **Danny Ketch**.
